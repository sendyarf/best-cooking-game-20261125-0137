**Ember & Thyme** is a cozy, fast-paced cooking game set in a small bistro kitchen. You are the chef on a 3-minute lunch shift: tickets land on the order board with a patience bar that drains while you work, so every second counts. Grab ingredients from the pantry, chop vegetables on the boards, grill patties on the stove (and pull them off before they burn!), build each dish on a plate at the pass, and ring the bell at the serve window to send it out. Serving fast keeps your combo alive and your score climbing; a missed ticket breaks the combo. Orders arrive faster and grow more complex as the shift heats up — Garden Salad gives way to Deluxe Burgers — and the shift ends with your final score, dishes served, and a star rating from Bistro Legend down to a tough shift.

## How to play

- **Mouse:** click a station to walk over and use it — click the pantry to pick up an ingredient, a chopping board to chop it, the stove to grill a patty, a plate to add food to it, and the serve window to deliver a finished plate.
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrow keys move the chef; **E** or **Space** uses the nearest station; **1–6** grab an ingredient from the pantry (tomato, lettuce, onion, patty, bun, cheese); **R** restarts a shift; **Enter** starts.
- **Rules of the kitchen:** the chef carries one thing at a time. A plate only serves when it matches a ticket exactly. Burnt food goes in the bin. Watch the patience bars — a ticket that empties is a missed order and your combo resets.
- **Screenshots:** open `game/index.html` with `?shot=cover`, `?shot=orders`, `?shot=cooking`, or `?shot=serve` to stage a scene.

Built with vanilla JavaScript and Canvas 2D — no libraries, no network, no build step. Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser and cook.
